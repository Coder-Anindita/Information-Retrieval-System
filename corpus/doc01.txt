Information retrieval is the process of obtaining relevant documents from a large collection based on a user query. Unlike database search, retrieval deals mostly with unstructured text such as articles, emails and web pages. A retrieval system must understand what the user is looking for and return the most useful results quickly. Early systems were used in libraries to locate books and research papers. Today information retrieval powers web search, digital libraries, e-commerce product search and enterprise document management. The field combines ideas from computer science, linguistics, statistics and information science.
