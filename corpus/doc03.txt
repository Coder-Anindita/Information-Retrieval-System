Tokenization is the first step in processing raw text for indexing. It splits a stream of characters into individual units called tokens, which are usually words. Simple tokenizers break text on whitespace and punctuation, but real text contains tricky cases. Hyphenated words, apostrophes, email addresses, dates and numbers all require special rules. Different languages also need different tokenizers, since languages like Chinese do not use spaces between words. The same tokenizer must be applied to both documents and queries so that terms match correctly during search.
