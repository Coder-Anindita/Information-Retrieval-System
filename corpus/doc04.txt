Case folding is a normalization technique that converts all letters to lowercase. With case folding, the words Apple, APPLE and apple are all treated as the same term. This improves recall because users often type queries in lowercase regardless of how words appear in documents. However, case folding can sometimes lose meaning, for example when distinguishing the company Apple from the fruit apple. Other normalization steps include removing accents, deleting hyphens and handling abbreviations like U.S.A. Normalization creates equivalence classes of terms so that small spelling differences do not prevent a match.
