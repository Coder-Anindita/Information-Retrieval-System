Wildcard queries allow users to search for terms when they are unsure of the exact spelling. A trailing wildcard query such as comp* matches terms like computer, compiler and computation. A leading wildcard such as *tion matches words that end with those letters. Wildcards are also useful for finding different forms of the same word, like plural and singular versions. Supporting wildcards requires special indexes because a normal inverted index only allows exact term lookup. The system first expands the wildcard into matching vocabulary terms and then runs an OR query over them.
