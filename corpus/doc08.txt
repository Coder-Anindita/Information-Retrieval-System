Stemming is a technique that reduces words to their root form so that related words match each other. For example, running, runs and runner may all be reduced to run. The Porter stemmer is the most widely used stemming algorithm for English and works by applying a series of suffix-stripping rules. Stemming increases recall because a query for connect can also find connection and connected. However, aggressive stemming can join unrelated words and reduce precision. Lemmatization is a more careful alternative that uses a dictionary to find the correct base form of each word.
