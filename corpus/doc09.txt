Stop words are extremely common words such as the, is, of, and, to and in. Because they appear in almost every document, they carry very little information about what a document is about. Many retrieval systems remove stop words during indexing to save space and speed up query processing. A stop word list is usually created by sorting terms by collection frequency and picking the most common ones. However, removing stop words can cause problems for phrase queries like to be or not to be. Modern web search engines often keep stop words because compression makes their storage cost small.
