Machine learning has transformed how search engines rank documents. Instead of hand-tuning ranking formulas, engineers train models on data about which results users found useful. This approach is called learning to rank, and it combines many features such as term matches, page quality and click behavior. User clicks provide valuable feedback, although they are noisy and biased toward top results. Deep learning models like BERT can now understand the meaning of queries rather than just matching keywords. These neural ranking models have greatly improved results for long and conversational queries.
