TF-IDF is a weighting scheme used to measure how important a term is to a document in a collection. Term frequency counts how often a term appears in a document, so frequent terms get higher weight. Inverse document frequency reduces the weight of terms that appear in many documents across the collection. The final score multiplies these two values, giving high scores to terms that are frequent in a document but rare overall. For example, the word retrieval in an article about search gets a high weight, while common words get a low weight. TF-IDF remains a strong baseline for document ranking and text classification.
