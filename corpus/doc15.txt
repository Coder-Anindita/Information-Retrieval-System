A posting list stores the document identifiers for a single term in the inverted index. Each entry in the list is called a posting and may also include the term frequency and positions of the term in that document. Posting lists are usually kept sorted by document identifier so that they can be merged efficiently. Intersecting two sorted lists for an AND query takes time proportional to the sum of their lengths. Skip pointers can make intersection even faster by jumping over postings that cannot match. For large collections, posting lists are compressed using techniques like gap encoding and variable byte codes.
