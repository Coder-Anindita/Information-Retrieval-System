Natural language processing is a branch of artificial intelligence that allows computers to understand and process human language text. Common tasks include part-of-speech tagging, named entity recognition, sentiment analysis and machine translation. In information retrieval, natural language processing helps with tokenization, stemming, query understanding and question answering. Modern language models learn word meanings from huge amounts of text and represent words as dense vectors called embeddings. These embeddings capture similarity, so words like car and automobile end up close together. This enables semantic search, where results match the meaning of a query and not just its exact words.
