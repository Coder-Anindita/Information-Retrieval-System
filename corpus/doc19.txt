Computer networks connect devices so that data can travel across the internet and local networks. Data is broken into small packets that are sent through routers and switches to reach their destination. Protocols like TCP and IP define how packets are addressed, transmitted and reassembled. The HTTP protocol, which runs on top of TCP, is used by web browsers to request pages from web servers. Network speed and reliability are important for search engines, since they serve millions of queries every second. Data centers use high-speed networks to connect thousands of servers that store and search the index.
